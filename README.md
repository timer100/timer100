# 👋 Hi, I'm Tim

I'm currently a graduate student at the Graduate Institute of Photonics and Optoelectronics, National Taiwan University (NTU), in Taiwan.
My current work focuses on numerical simulation methods, including modeling topological matter and simulating optical systems with the finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) method.
I also explore AI technologies, including custom agent skills, AI-assisted workflows, and AI image generation.
I enjoy turning physical models into code and making the results easier to understand.

## 👤 About me

- **Scientific computing:** building simulations, checking numerical results, and sharing examples through notebooks.
- **Photonics:** photonic crystal waveguides, FDTD simulations, and Fourier optics.
- **Topological matter:** electronic states and high-order harmonic generation (HHG).
- **AI:** exploring AI workflows, creating custom agent skills, and applying AI to scientific computing.
- **AI image generation:** exploring character generation, dataset preparation, and local LoRA training workflows with SDXL, Z-Image, and ComfyUI.
